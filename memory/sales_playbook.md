## 1. One-Page Live Demo Script (10 minutes)

**Use when:** discovery call, in-person shed visit, video pitch.

### Setup (30 sec)
> "Before I show you anything — quick question: how are you tracking silo feed levels right now? Paper? Excel? Nothing? … OK, in the next 8 minutes I'll show you the exact tool a working broiler grower built to kill that paperwork. If it's not for you, we hang up and no hard feelings."

### Show 1 — The pain (60 sec) — share screen
Open a **blank Excel spreadsheet** from the Ingham's / Baiada template.
> "This is what most growers still use. Every morning: walk sheds, remember numbers, come back to the office, type them into cells. Miss a docket, you're chasing pallets. Two weeks in, the sheet is out of date. Sound familiar?"

### Show 2 — Walk the sheds (2 min) — phone screen
Open the **Silo Reader** on your phone (broilerbasemate.com.au/reader?farm=demo).
> "Now the grower's phone. Scan the shed QR, three silo boxes appear, tap in the tonnes, hit Save. That's it. **8 seconds per shed group.**"
Save 3 sheds live.
> "See the green tick? That reading just synced to the Feed Program cloud spreadsheet — head office sees it instantly."

### Show 3 — AI Docket (60 sec) — phone
Snap a photo of an Ingham's docket (or use a demo one).
> "Truck rolls up. Grower snaps a photo of the docket. AI reads it — feed type, kg, docket #, plant — auto-filled into the Feed Program. No typing. Detects Ingham's, Baiada, BPL, and generic dockets."

### Show 4 — Farm Buddy AI (90 sec) — desktop
Switch to Feed Program → Farm Buddy banner.
> "This is the brain. Farm Buddy reads every silo reading + every incoming feed order + FCR curve, then tells you **which shed to drop the next load into, and when the truck absolutely must arrive.** Not 'looks low' — 'Shed 3&4 runs out 3:20pm Thursday, drop 22t there'. Growers using it are cutting truck emergencies from 3–4 per batch to zero."

### Show 5 — End of Batch (90 sec)
Open EOB Report.
> "Batch is done. One click. Every KPI your integrator wants — CFCR, FCR, cost/kg, mortality %, weight gain, feed cost — auto-calculated in a PDF that matches the processor's sheet exactly. Emailed to head office in under 30 seconds. Growers tell me this saves them 3–4 hours per batch."

### Close (60 sec)
> "So — 14-day free trial, no card. If it clicks, plans start at $25/mo for up to 6 sheds. Founding Grower deal: 50% off forever + a direct line to me. Only 25 spots. Want me to set your account up now while we're on the call?"

**Objection cheat-sheet**
- *"Too expensive."* → "It's less than half a shed-worth of feed wastage per year. First truck emergency it prevents pays for 6 months."
- *"I'm not techy."* → "That's exactly why I built it. Punch numbers, hit Save. If you can text, you can use it."
- *"I'll think about it."* → "Sure — but the Founding Grower price locks in for life. Once we hit 25 farms, that door closes. Can I hold a spot for 48 hours?"

## 5. Sales Follow-Up Cadence (SOP)

**Golden rule:** ≥ 6 touches over 21 days before you mark a lead lost.

| Touch | Day | Channel      | What to send                                                                                        |
|-------|-----|--------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1     | 0   | Email (cold) | Section 3 template A/B/C/D depending on audience                                                    |
| 2     | 2   | SMS          | "Josh here — sent you a note re Broiler Base Mate on Mon. Any interest? 8-min demo?"                |
| 3     | 5   | Email        | Short-form: "Landed you a spot on the founder call this Fri 10am. Y/N?" + 90-sec video link         |
| 4     | 9   | Phone call   | Best effort — talk about *their* pain (feed waste? EOB paperwork?), then demo                       |
| 5     | 14  | Email        | Social proof: "Here's what [Founding grower X] said after 2 weeks…" + [screenshot testimonial]      |
| 6     | 21  | Email        | Break-up: "Sounds like now's not the right time. I'll close your file — reply STAY if I've got that wrong." |

**Post-close:**
- Trial start → automatic 4-email onboarding sequence (already built in the app)
- Day 3 of trial → founder personal call/text "how's it going?"
- Day 10 of trial → founder call: convert or extend
- Day 30 paying → ask for testimonial or case study (Section 7)

## 6. Checkout / Trial Journey Audit — Drop-off Points

Reviewed the flow live: `/api/checkout` → Stripe → success → magic-link email → login → onboarding.

| # | Step                                          | Drop-off risk    | Fix / recommendation                                                                 |
|---|-----------------------------------------------|------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | Landing hero → clicks "Start Free Trial"      | 🟢 Low           | Now leads with Founding Grower deal (this deploy). Keep as-is.                       |
| 2 | Plan card → **Buyer Details Modal** appears   | 🟡 **HIGH**      | Modal asks for full name + email BEFORE Stripe. Cuts conversion ~20-40%. **Fix:** drop the modal — Stripe already collects email. Pre-fill from `?g=slug` cookie if available. |
| 3 | Stripe checkout page                          | 🟢 Low           | Live-mode Stripe, AUD, tested — 4 packages working. Keep.                            |
| 4 | Post-payment `/landing/success` page          | 🟡 Medium        | Currently plain "thank you" — **add:** big "Open your app" button + "Book onboarding call" calendar embed. |
| 5 | Magic link email — 30-60 sec delay            | 🔴 **CRITICAL**  | If growers close the tab before the email arrives, they never come back. **Fix:** on success page, show a live countdown + resend button + polling for magic link ("Click here as soon as your email arrives"). Also add a "Send to my phone" SMS fallback via Twilio (optional). |
| 6 | First login → Feed Program empty              | 🟡 Medium        | Empty state can feel broken. **Fix:** show a full-screen "5-minute setup wizard": (a) name your farm, (b) enable shed groups, (c) enter placement date, (d) drop your first QR poster PDF. Currently exists as a settings screen — needs to be forced/promoted on first login. |
| 7 | Day 3 of trial → grower hasn't logged in      | 🔴 **HIGH**      | Silent churn. **Fix:** cron job — if `farm.lastLoginAt < now - 3d && trialActive`, founder gets a Slack ping to call them personally. Also fire an auto-email: "Need help getting started? Book a 15-min call: [Calendly]". |
| 8 | Day 14 → trial ends, card charge fails        | 🟡 Medium        | Stripe handles dunning already. **Add:** founder gets a Slack ping on any failed charge so a personal follow-up call goes out same day. |

**Priority stack:**
1. **P0** — Kill the Buyer Details Modal (fastest conversion win)
2. **P0** — Fix the magic-link handoff (live countdown, polling, resend)
3. **P1** — Force the 5-minute setup wizard on first login
4. **P1** — Day-3 no-login founder alert
5. **P2** — Failed-charge founder alert

## 7. Founding Grower Case-Study Template

**Length:** 350–450 words. Publish on landing page + LinkedIn + trade press.

### Structure

**Headline (1 line):**
> "[Farm name] cut EOB paperwork from 4 hours to 4 minutes — and stopped every truck emergency last batch."

**Grower snapshot (2 lines):**
> [Farm name] · [Region] · [# sheds] · Integrator: [Ingham's/Baiada/BPL] · Grower for [X] years · Placed [Y] birds/batch

**The problem — before BBM (60 words):**
> "[Direct quote about the paperwork/feed-tracking mess. Should sound like a grower complaining at the pub. Example: 'Every batch I'd be up till 11pm punching silo numbers into a spreadsheet the missus said looked like a war-crime. And I'd still get chased by the mill 'cause I forgot a docket.']"

**What changed (80 words):**
> Grower signed up on [date]. Within [X days] they were using [feature 1], [feature 2], [feature 3]. Include one concrete measurable number:
> - **EOB report time:** 4 hours → 4 minutes
> - **Truck emergencies last batch:** 3 → 0
> - **Feed variance vs planned:** ±8% → ±1.5%
> - **Mortality reported on time:** 60% → 100%

**Founder-favourite grower quote (60 words):**
> Pull out the punchiest line from the interview. Bold + oversized on the page.
> Example: "**I paid for a whole year in the first week — one truck emergency I didn't have.**"

**The verdict (2 lines):**
> Recommend BBM? Yes/No/Conditional. What tier they're on. Their referral link.

**Call to action:**
> "Want the same? [Founding Grower page →]"

### Interview questions (email/text before the write-up)

1. Before BBM — what were you using? How much time did feed tracking + EOB take you per batch?
2. What was the moment you knew it was working?
3. What one number changed the most? (Feed variance, truck emergencies, EOB time, mortality reporting…)
4. Who else on your farm uses it? Feed truck driver? Missus? Kids?
5. What would you say to a mate who's on the fence?
6. Anything you wish it did that it doesn't yet?
