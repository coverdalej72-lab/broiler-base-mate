Simple enough to use on day one.

No installation. No training. Open the link, log silo readings, scan a docket, and you're managing a flock from your phone.

1

📲 Scan to open

Scan the QR in your Feed Program. The mobile reader opens — Add to Home Screen and it's an app.

2

📋 Tap silo readings

Walk the sheds. Type each silo's tonnes left. One tap saves all three silos in a shed group.

3

🚚 Scan dockets on arrival

Photo or QR — Ingham's, Baiada, BPL all auto-detected. Feed type, kg, docket # filled instantly.

4

📊 Spreadsheet auto-updates

Within 2 minutes, the Feed Program's End-of-Batch section reflects every reading and delivery — no copying.

Common questions.

How long is the free trial?+
14 days, full access to every feature in your chosen tier. No credit card needed at signup — we only ask for payment if you decide to stay.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes. One-click cancel from your account page. Your data stays accessible for 90 days afterwards so you can export it.
Does it work on iPhone AND Android?+
Yes. Both work the same way — no app store install needed. Open the link in any browser, tap 'Add to Home Screen', and it lives on your phone like a real app.
Is my data secure?+
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest in MongoDB Atlas. Payments are processed entirely by Stripe — we never see your card details.
What if I have more than 30 sheds?+
Talk to us! The Integrator plan is $60/farm/month with volume pricing for 5+ farms. We've already wired multi-farm support into the codebase.
Do you support Ingham's, Baiada, AND BPL Adelaide dockets?+
Yes — all three. The AI auto-detects the supplier from the docket header. If you've got a docket from a smaller mill, send us a photo and we'll add it.
Can my workers use it without an account?+
Yes. They scan a QR code that opens the field reader. No login needed on the phone — just open and tap silo readings.
Do I keep my data if I cancel?+
Always. Export to CSV/Excel anytime. After cancelling, your data stays exportable for 90 days.
