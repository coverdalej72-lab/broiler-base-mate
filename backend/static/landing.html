See it in action.

The grower's phone and the office desktop, in perfect sync — real farms, real data, right now.

01 · DESKTOP
The Feed Program — every batch, every shed.

Auto-cascading formulas replace your integrator's Excel: Feed Alloc, Usage, Feed on Hand, Silo Total — updated the moment a grower saves a silo reading from their phone. Click any yellow cell to edit. One-click End-of-Batch PDF matches your processor's sheet exactly.

02 · MOBILE
The Silo Reader — 8 seconds per shed.
  • Open the reader on your phone — every shed group is listed.
  • Tap the tonnes for Silo A/B/C, hit Save — three silos in one press.
  • Farm Buddy warns you the moment any silo goes red.
  • Every reading syncs to head office in under 2 minutes.
  • Add to Home Screen — behaves like a native app, works offline.
🧠 What the phone screenshot shows
Farm Buddy sitting at the top: "ORDER FEED NOW — Sheds 19 & 20 only has 3.0 t left". Below it: four shed groups (1&2, 3&4, 5&6, 7&8) with silos A/B/C filled in — 22.5, 18.0, 24.2 tonnes at a glance.
03 · AI FEATURES

Four AI helpers, one app.

Every AI feature does the same thing — takes something you used to type or count, and does it from a photo instead.

📸
DOCKET SCAN
Snap the driver's carbon-paper docket. AI reads Ingham's, Baiada or BPL delivery slips and adds the tonnes to the right silo automatically.
⚖️
WEIGH BIRDS · PER SHED
Snap a bird on any surface. AI estimates the live weight in grams, per shed. Or type it in manually — either way, it flows into Flock Forecast on the desktop.
🐣
CHICK COUNTER
Chick truck arrived? Snap each crate — AI counts the day-olds. Verify placement against the hatchery docket before the driver leaves.
📄
MORT SHEET · AI
Photo the paper mort sheet. AI reads today's row and auto-updates "birds left" in every shed. Kills 20 minutes of typing at the end of the walk.

Common questions.

How long is the free trial?+
30 days, full access to every feature in your chosen tier. No credit card needed at signup — we only ask for payment if you decide to stay.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Yes. One-click cancel from your account page. Your data stays accessible for 90 days afterwards so you can export it.
Does it work on iPhone AND Android?+
Yes. Both work the same way — no app store install needed. Open the link in any browser, tap 'Add to Home Screen', and it lives on your phone like a real app.
Is my data secure?+
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest in MongoDB Atlas. Payments are processed entirely by Stripe — we never see your card details.
What if I have more than 30 sheds?+
Talk to us! The Integrator plan is $60/farm/month with volume pricing for 5+ farms. We've already wired multi-farm support into the codebase.
Do you support Ingham's, Baiada, AND BPL Adelaide dockets?+
Yes — all three. The AI auto-detects the supplier from the docket header. If you've got a docket from a smaller mill, send us a photo and we'll add it.
Can my workers use it without an account?+
Yes. You share a private reader link with them — no login needed on the phone. Just open and tap silo readings.
Do I keep my data if I cancel?+
Always. Export to CSV/Excel anytime. After cancelling, your data stays exportable for 90 days.

Why Australian broiler growers switched from paper & Excel

📐 Processor-exact maths

FCR, corrected FCR (0.27 slope, 2.45 kg standard), cage rating, efficiency rating and grower payment — the same formulas Baiada and Ingham use to settle your batch. Live to three decimals before the truck leaves.

📷 Snap a docket, walk away

Photo any feed docket — Ingham's, Baiada, BPL Adelaide, Riverina, Manildra — AI reads supplier, feed type, product code, tonnage, delivery date and ticket number. Drops straight into your feed history.

📡 Works with bad reception

The mobile reader queues silo readings on-device if 4G drops out, then syncs the moment you're back near Wi-Fi. Never lose a reading again.

🧠 Farm Buddy AI advisor

Looks at every shed's bird count, day-age, projected weight and remaining feed — then tells you which shed to drop the next truck into and how long your silos will last.

🚚 End-of-batch in one click

When the last truck rolls off, your EOB PDF is already done — feed left, mortality breakdown, batch costs, ready to email to your supervisor. Done in the time it takes to boil a kettle.

🏘 Multi-farm for Ops Managers

Running 4, 8 or 20 farms? Ops Dashboard rolls every farm's batches, mortality, feed usage and grower chats into one screen. Run the whole region from your kitchen bench.
