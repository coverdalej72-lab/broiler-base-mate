01 · DESKTOP
The Feed Program — every batch, every shed.

Auto-cascading formulas replace your integrator's Excel: Feed Alloc, Usage, Feed on Hand, Silo Total — updated the moment a grower saves a silo reading from their phone. Click any yellow cell to edit. One-click End-of-Batch PDF matches your processor's sheet exactly.

02 · MOBILE
The Silo Reader — 8 seconds per shed.
  • Open the reader on your phone — every shed group is listed.
  • Tap the tonnes for Silo A/B/C, hit Save — three silos in one press.
  • Farm Buddy warns you the moment any silo goes red.
  • Every reading syncs to head office in under 2 minutes.
  • Add to Home Screen — behaves like a native app, works offline.

FCR, corrected FCR (0.27 slope, 2.45 kg standard), cage rating, efficiency rating and grower payment — the same formulas Baiada and Ingham use to settle your batch. Live to three decimals before the truck leaves.
