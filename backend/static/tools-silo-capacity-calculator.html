The formula

Cylinder volume (m³) = π × (diameter / 2)² × cylinder height

Cone volume (m³) = (1/3) × π × (diameter / 2)² × cone height

Feed weight (kg) = (cylinder + cone volume) × bulk density (kg/m³)

Days of feed left = feed weight (kg) ÷ daily consumption (kg/day)
