The formula

Cage rating = 39.5 ÷ corrected age (days)

Efficiency rating = ((1.78 ÷ cFCR) × 0.7) + (cage rating × 0.3)

Payment per bird = efficiency rating × payment factor

Total batch $ = payment per bird × birds caught

How to improve your grower payment

1 · Cut FCR by cutting feed wastage

Every 0.05 improvement in cFCR is worth roughly A$0.001–0.002 per bird — for a 45,000 bird batch that's $45–$90. Broiler Base Mate's Farm Buddy AI recommends drop timing per shed to keep silos accurate and avoid over-fill.

2 · Lower your corrected batch age

Catching a day earlier at the same weight bumps your cage rating. This is why hot-weeks or cold-weeks matter — track shed temperature and daily gain to spot lagging sheds early.

3 · Keep morts down

Morts don't just reduce birds caught — they also compound because feed already eaten by dead birds inflates FCR. Broiler Base Mate's mort-sheet AI catches this within hours instead of at settlement.
