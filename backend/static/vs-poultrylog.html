Where PoultryLog is the right choice

PoultryLog is a solid mobile-first daily-walkthrough tool. If you only need morts, culls, water, weather and equipment checks — and your paperwork is otherwise managed on paper or in Excel — PoultryLog does that well and doesn't ask you to change how you handle dockets.

It's especially light if you don't want AI in your workflow.

Where Broiler Base Mate pulls ahead

1 · Kill the docket paperwork

The AI docket scanner reads Ingham's, Baiada and BPL Adelaide dockets from a phone photo — supplier, feed type, kg, docket number, silo destination — and drops the delivery straight into the End-of-Batch sheet. No typing.

2 · Live FCR, not batch-end FCR

Because dockets and silo readings flow in real-time, cFCR, cage rating and efficiency rating recompute every day. You catch a bad shed on day 12, not at settlement.

3 · One-tap branded End-of-Batch PDF

A branded navy/gold report — matching the processor Excel layout — is emailed to grower, integrator and auditor with a single tap. See the landing page for a preview.

4 · Multi-farm Ops Manager view

If you run more than one farm or supervise growers, the Gold plan includes a multi-farm dashboard, remote settings push and in-app grower chat.
